Where the Sea Rises in Elegance

Wimbi

Inspired by the powerful high tides of the Tanzanian sea, Wimbi, meaning wave in Swahili, captures the untamed rhythm of nature in motion. Its sculptural design mirrors the fluid crest of waves rising and falling with grace.

“The moment just before a wave breaks.”

Gem Experience, on Wimbi

Rhythm, rarity and refined beauty

It is a tribute to the elegance of nature in motion, where every curve tells a story of rhythm, rarity and refined beauty.

The difficulty in a piece like this is the repetition: every crest has to match every other one, or the eye finds the one that does not.

Twenty-one carats of rhodolite

21.86 carats of octagon-shaped rose-coloured rhodolite, carefully set to evoke the moment just before a wave breaks: bold, breathtaking, and full of life.

The rhodolite's unique hue reflects both strength and softness, like the sea itself at sunset.
