What is Tanzanite?

The Maasai tell of a lightning bolt that struck the earth and lit the rocks a brilliant blue. That, they say, is how Tanzanite was made.

How was Tanzanite Born?

The Earth Moved

The Tanzanite's genesis lies in the tumultuous pan-African movement of tectonic plates, birthing a unique and precious stone.

A Bolt of Lightning

A lightning bolt struck the earth and lit the rocks a brilliant blue. That, the Maasai say, is how Tanzanite was made.

One Hill on Earth

There is only one source of high-quality Tanzanite in the world: a few square kilometres at the foot of Kilimanjaro, in northern Tanzania.

What is Inside a Tanzanite?

Trichroic

[adjective]

Showing three different colours when seen from three different directions.

Tanzanite is trichroic: turn it and the colour moves from blue to violet-blue, with a hint of burgundy.

A One-Generation Stone

1,000×

Scarcer than diamonds

Word of the stone spread quickly, and its rarity drew buyers from around the world. As there is only one source of high-quality Tanzanite, experts estimate that its supply may run out within a few decades, earning it the name of a “one-generation stone”.
