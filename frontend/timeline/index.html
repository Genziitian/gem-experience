The House

Timeline

From Color Clarity Ltd. in 1998 to Gem Experience today.

  1. 1998

    Color Clarity Ltd. is born

    Color Clarity Ltd. was born from the earth below, with a passion to bring the riches of the earth to the world. The miners begin digging deep into the earth's core to find a gem of rare beauty, tanzanite. The process includes the identification of a deposit, the extraction of the rough stone, and manufacturing. With every piece, a story untold; the birth of this brand will forever hold.

  2. 2007

    Tsavorite and spinel

    Our mining operations led to an exciting discovery: we had the privilege to discover two extraordinary precious stones in Tanzania. Tsavorite, a vibrant green gemstone, and spinel, a lesser-known gemstone with a range of impressive colours, were unearthed.

  3. 2010

    Jewellery for a royal family

    The creation of the first jewellery for an African royal family marks a remarkable moment in the history of Color Clarity Ltd. It used the rosy hue of pink tanzanite, a precious stone not only stunning to behold but also incredibly rare, making it a prized possession and coveted treasure, and a reminder of the rich cultural heritage of African royalty.

  4. 2015

    The Tanzanite Experience

    The beginning of an exciting year, with the launch of a new brand, The Tanzanite Experience: bringing beauty and style, a symbol of innovation, and a sparkle into the air. A journey begins with promise and care.

  5. 2018

    Dubai

    In Dubai's bustling city, the hum of our business intensifies and the latest operation gets underway, with promise and purpose, shining so bright. A journey begins, with hope and delight, bringing innovation to a world so bright. A new chapter unfolds, in the land of light.

  6. 2019

    A new store opens

    A new store opens, with a glistening shine, a milestone reached for this brand divine. Ten doors now stand, for all to see, a symbol of growth, for you and me. The future shines bright for our brand, so fine.

  7. 2022

    Gem Experience

    A new brand, Gem Experience, begins with hope and dreams, bringing fresh ideas with vibrant gleams. A journey unfolds, with passion and fire, inspiring all with its unique desire. A future awaits; our brand is so new, shining bright, like the morning dew.
