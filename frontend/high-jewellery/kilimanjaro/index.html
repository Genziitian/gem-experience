Ocean Wave

A wave in white gold, carrying green through its length. The form is the same rhythm as the sea it is named for, drawn flat around the collarbone.

“The shape of water, held still.”

Gem Experience, on Ocean Wave

Drawn flat

The wave is worked so that it sits against the body rather than standing away from it, which is what allows a piece of this width to be worn without weight.

Diamond follows the inside of every curve, so the line is lit from within the shape rather than outlined around it.

Green, through the curve

Green stones in pear and cushion cuts are set into the hollows of the wave, where the metal turns and the light collects.

White gold throughout, which leaves the green to do all of the colour.
