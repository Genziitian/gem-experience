Georgie

The Georgie Necklace is a tribute to Grace Kelly's portrayal of Georgie Elgin in The Country Girl. The necklace is drawn from Georgie's resilience and her quiet elegance.

Gem Experience, on Georgie

Warmth, in rose gold

Crafted in rose gold, the necklace reflects Georgie's warmth, while the 19 natural pearls represent her inner light and unwavering dignity.

The pearls run the length of the strand and the tanzanite interrupts them at intervals, laid along the line rather than across it.

Pearl and marquise tanzanite

19 natural pearls, with 12.35 carats of marquise-shaped tanzanite introducing a vibrant touch and symbolising the emotional complexity of Georgie's character.

The marquise cut enhances the gemstone's brilliance, much like Grace Kelly's luminous performance.
