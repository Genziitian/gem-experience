Bloom

Cycles of life. Every person blooms differently.

Just as a flower is cultivated for its beauty, the effort it takes to get to that stage often goes unnoticed. From sowing the seed, to becoming a sapling and eventually blooming into a flower.

Drawing a parallel to life: the day we achieve our goal, we take a moment to look back and appreciate the challenges, effort and patience it took to get there. Hence, Bloom.

The different gemstones represent that every person has a different way of blooming. Mint garnet for quiet strength, growth and harmony. Aquamarine for clarity, courage and calmness. Tanzanite for new beginnings, the rarity of life's special moments, and wisdom. Pink spinel for healing, rebirth and love.

Stones used: mint garnet, aquamarine, tanzanite and pink spinel. Set in white gold.

Safar

A journey through time. Designed with Shantanu Garg.

Safar means journey. The collection travels through time, from the architecture of India's past to the way it is worn today.

Influenced by the geometry, symmetry and intricate ornamentation of temple and Mughal architecture, and developed in collaboration with Shantanu Garg, each piece transforms traditional motifs into fluid, contemporary forms, reinterpreting India's architectural vocabulary into a modern, wearable expression.

The pieces are structured but easy to wear, and many of them transform, changing with the wearer while keeping the lines of the buildings they come from.

The movement is in the meaning as much as the form: design carried from one culture to another and drawn again for now.
