Flamenco

Two thousand one hundred hours

The centrepiece is a necklace of 34.56 carats of pink spinel, and 2,100 hours of work. The stones are rare and vivid, and they graduate in size down the length of the piece.

Just as a dancer's skirt swirls in rippling waves of fabric, the design curves and flares with movement, highlighting the brilliance of the stones.

Soft purple and blue

The companion set carries a harmonious interplay of soft purple and blue tones in white gold: a vivid 20.36 carat aquamarine, paired with 25.18 carats of fancy lavender tanzanite and 19.97 carats of blue tanzanites.

Between them run 24.53 carats of diamonds, set to follow the turn of the dancer. In all, the piece took more than 3,700 hours to make.

The suite

Flamenco is made in two sets, each a necklace with its earrings: pink spinel, and aquamarine with tanzanite.
