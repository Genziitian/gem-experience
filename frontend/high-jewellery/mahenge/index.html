Samaah

Channeling the fiery soul of Flamenco, Samaah places a 16.05 carat heart-shaped pink tourmaline at its centre, radiating duende and intensity.

“The piece transforms gemstones into the very poetry of the dance.”

Gem Experience, on Samaah

Twenty-six hundred hours

A cascade of 32.10 carats of brilliant-cut diamonds unfurls across the neckline, echoing the flare of fabric in its final flourish. It is a celebration of culture and artistry of 2,600 hours.

From the vibrant centre, diamond rays burst outward like the dancer's skirt mid-turn, each line alive with rhythm, movement, and grace.

The soul of the jewel

At the heart blazes a 16.05 carat heart-shaped pink tourmaline, its vivid hue the soul of the jewel and the anchor of the composition.

The name is the samaah itself: the sweeping flight of a dancer's skirt, translated into brilliance that moves as she does.

In the room

Samaah translates rhythm, movement and grace into luminous form. The diamonds cascade outward like a dancer's swirling skirt, so the piece reads differently with every turn of the body.
