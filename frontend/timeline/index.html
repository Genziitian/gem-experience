The House

Timeline

The house's journey, and the stones that shaped it.

  1. Over thirty years ago

    Sourcing begins in East Africa

    The house starts choosing rare coloured stones directly at the source, in Tanzania.

  2. 1990s

    Malaya garnet is found

    Local miners find Malaya garnet in the Umba River Valley, straddling Tanzania and Kenya; it joins the stones we work with.

  3. Today

    Ateliers in Arusha, Dubai and Jaipur

    Pieces are shown in private at our three ateliers, and the stones are cut in our own workshop.

  4. Today

    High Jewellery, counted in hours

    One-of-a-kind pieces such as Flamenco, over 3,700 hours at the bench, alongside the Fine Jewellery collections.
