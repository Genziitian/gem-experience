Weaver

The intricate overlapping pattern in the piece is inspired by the meticulous weaves in a bird's nest. An ode to nature's creative brilliance, reflecting the fine details of the world around us.

“Each link symbolises delicate balance and flow in the design.”

Gem Experience, on Weaver

Woven, as a nest is woven

This takes a deep dive into the collection, Dancing Weaver. The overlapping links pass through one another rather than meeting at a joint, so the collar moves as one piece.

Every curve is set along its whole length, including the sections that face inward and are only seen as the piece turns.

Twenty-eight carats of tanzanite

28.25 carats of round-shaped tanzanite, with brilliant-cut diamonds.

The stones fall at the close of each link, so the blue reads as punctuation along the weave rather than as a row of settings.
